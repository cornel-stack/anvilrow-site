Three people, one workshop

Anvilrow is a private limited company registered in Kenya in August 2026. Three of us started it because we kept seeing the same pattern: good ideas left unbuilt for want of a team that could handle data, design, and engineering without the handoffs.

We split our time between products we own and projects we take on for others. The split keeps us honest — when we advise a client on a technical decision, we are making the same kind of decisions on our own products that same week. Nothing gets theoretical.
